SECTION "B" — Short Answer Questions (40 Marks)
Q2 — Answer any FIVE Questions (10 Marks)
(i) How did Caliph settle the dispute?

The Caliph (Hazrat Omer R.A) wisely settled the dispute by asking the rich man to carry the beggar on his back for a short distance, just as the beggar had ridden the rich man's horse. This way, both parties were given equal treatment and justice was served fairly.

(ii) Why was Uncle Inayat liked by the children?

Uncle Inayat was loved by children because he was a kind, warm-hearted, and caring person. He always treated children with affection and respect, told them interesting stories, and never made them feel ignored or unimportant.

(iii) How did the spider finally reach its cobweb?

The spider kept trying again and again despite failing many times. Through its determination and continuous effort, it finally succeeded in reaching its cobweb. This story teaches us the lesson of patience and perseverance.

(iv) How is social media affecting relationships?

Social media is negatively affecting human relationships. People spend more time on their phones than with family and friends, which leads to emotional distance and lack of real communication. It has reduced face-to-face interaction and weakened personal bonds.

(v) Why did Asif and his family sleep in a tent?

Asif and his family had to sleep in a tent because the hotel or guesthouse they had planned to stay at was fully booked or unavailable. As a result, they had to manage with a tent as temporary shelter during their trip.

(vi) Two important safety measures to save lives:

1. Always wear a helmet while riding a motorcycle or bicycle to protect yourself from head injuries.
2. Always fasten your seatbelt while travelling in a car to prevent serious injury during accidents.

Q7 — Essay: Problems of Karachi (10 Marks)
Problems of Karachi

Karachi is the largest city of Pakistan and the economic hub of the country. It is home to millions of people from all over Pakistan. However, despite its importance, Karachi faces numerous serious problems that affect the daily life of its citizens.

One of the biggest problems is the shortage of water. Millions of residents do not receive clean drinking water regularly. People have to buy water from tankers at high prices. This is a serious issue that needs immediate attention.

Another major problem is load shedding of electricity and gas. Citizens face long hours without power and gas, making life very difficult especially in extreme weather. Industries also suffer due to these shortages.

Traffic congestion is also a major issue in Karachi. The roads are overcrowded and there is no proper public transport system. As a result, people waste hours in traffic jams every day.

Law and order situation, street crimes, and poor sanitation are other challenges the city faces. Garbage is often not collected for days, which spreads disease.

In conclusion, the government must take serious steps to solve these problems. Proper planning, investment in infrastructure, and honest leadership are the only way to make Karachi a better city.

Q9 — Comprehension Passage (15 Marks)
(i) Why is it necessary to have good communication skills? (2 Marks)

Good communication skills are necessary because people judge, evaluate, and promote others based on how well they communicate. Having strong communication skills helps a person get ahead of colleagues and succeed in their career.

(ii) How can communication skills be developed? (2 Marks)

Communication skills can be developed by observing great communicators and adopting their styles. One should practice listening carefully, learn when to pause, avoid speaking too fast, and avoid unnecessary or emotional words. Writing formal emails correctly also helps improve communication.
