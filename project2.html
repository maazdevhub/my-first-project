PROJECT 02

Social Media Templates

Professional design templates for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and LinkedIn. Fully customizable and ready to use for businesses and creators.

Timeline

4 Weeks

Role

Graphic Designer

Industry

Social Media & Branding

Figma Social Media Design Content Creation Branding UI Design

Overview

A comprehensive collection of professionally designed social media templates created in Figma. These templates are designed for modern businesses and content creators who need to maintain a consistent visual identity across all social platforms.

Each template follows best practices for social media design, including proper spacing, typography, color theory, and platform-specific dimensions.

Template Platforms

📸 Instagram Posts

Square post templates optimized for Instagram feed with multiple design variations.

📱 Instagram Stories

Full-height story templates designed for engagement with eye-catching visuals.

👍 Facebook Posts

Landscape and square templates optimized for Facebook's feed algorithm.

💼 LinkedIn Posts

Professional templates for B2B content and thought leadership.

🎬 TikTok Covers

Vertical video cover designs optimized for TikTok's vertical format.

🖼️ Reels Thumbnails

Eye-catching thumbnail designs for Instagram and Facebook Reels.

Design Approach

Color Psychology: Carefully selected color palettes that evoke specific emotions and maintain brand consistency.

Typography: Modern, readable fonts that work well at small sizes on mobile devices.

Visual Hierarchy: Strategic placement of elements to guide user attention and encourage interaction.

Consistency: Unified design language across all platforms for better brand recognition.
